## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies a harmonious blend of botanical freshness, culinary vitality, and clinical reassurance. Designed for nutrition planning, personalized dietary management, and ingredient procurement, the UI balances rigorous health science with appetizing warmth. The emotional experience aims to inspire calm competence, culinary curiosity, and absolute trust in nutritional data.

### Design Movement: Organic Clinical Minimalism
The aesthetic bridges modern Nordic clarity and human-centered health technology:
- **Clean Botanical Canvas**: Pristine, light-flooded surfaces (`#FFFFFF`, `#F7FAF8`) accented by deep chlorophyll greens and warm citrus oranges.
- **Micro-Structured Information Hierarchy**: Dense nutritional data presented through clear typography, soft rings, and pill-based macro badges instead of sterile tabular sheets.
- **Safety-First Ergonomics**: Allergen alerts, diet compatibility scores, and medical disclaimers receive dedicated spatial elevation and redundant visual signifiers, avoiding ambiguous iconography or low-contrast text.

## Colors

### Chromatic Architecture
- **Primary Green (`#27A468`, `brand-500`)**: Governs core health confirmations, active states, progress rings, and focal points. Supported by `brand-800` (`#155439`) for authoritative contrast and `brand-50` (`#F0FBF4`) for tinted card highlights.
- **Accent Orange (`#F58A2B`, `accent-500`)**: Reserved strictly for appetite stimulation, commercial actions, checkout conversions, and grocery delivery triggers.
- **Ink Palette (Forest Neutrals)**: Avoids synthetic blacks. Grounded in `ink-900` (`#14201A`), a rich chlorophyll-charcoal delivering WCAG AAA contrast against light surfaces while maintaining visual warmth.

### Semantic & Data Visualization Rules
- **Verdict & Health Indicators**: Never convey health clearances through color alone. Pair background-foreground duos with explicit glyphs (`✓` for `ok`, `⚠` for `warn`, `⛔` for `danger`).
- **Macronutrient Tokens**: Fixed semantic tokens for nutrient donuts and bar charts:
  - Protein: `#3B82F6` (Cool Technical Blue)
  - Carbohydrates: `#F59E0B` (Grain Amber)
  - Healthy Fats: `#EC4899` (Nutritive Rose)
  - Dietary Fiber: `#10B981` (Sprout Mint)

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy & Readability
The typographic framework relies exclusively on `Be Vietnam Pro` for all levels, optimizing diacritic rendering and legibility across complex culinary terms and localized Vietnamese medical names. 

- **Display & Headlines**: Rendered in weights `600` and `700` using tight letter tracking (-0.015em) for punchy recipe titles, daily caloric goals, and nutritional overviews.
- **Body Core (`body-md`)**: Calibrated to `16px / 24px` on regular weight (`400`) to guarantee high-velocity readability during active cooking sessions.
- **Accessibility Scaler ("Chữ lớn")**: When user preferences activate large-text accessibility modes, `body-md` defaults shift smoothly to `18px`, and `body-lg` shifts to `20px` across all instructional and dietary cards.
- **Micro & Legal Tier**: `legal-micro` is reserved for mandatory medical disclaimers, FDA/MOH statements, and allergen contamination caveats at the base of nutritional breakdowns.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture
The design system operates on an uncompromising 4px sub-rhythm with an 8px macro scale:
- **Canvas Boundaries**: Mobile viewports utilize full-bleed fluid containers with a strict minimum width of `360px` and `margin: 1rem` (16px safe margin). Desktop views center inside a max target boundary of `1440px`.
- **Responsive Grids**:
  - **Mobile (360px - 767px)**: 4-column fluid layout, `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom sheets, floating meal summary bars, and single-column recipe cards.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `gutter-md: 1.5rem`. Split views pairing shopping baskets alongside scheduled meal days.
  - **Desktop (1024px - 1440px)**: 12-column layout, `gutter-lg: 2rem`. Multi-pane dashboard layouts displaying nutritional analytics, interactive weekly meal planners, and persistent e-commerce grocery checkouts side by side.

### Ergonomic Rules
- **Interactive Bounds**: All touch targets—including macro filters, counter steppers (`+`/`-`), and heart buttons—must maintain a hard minimum clickable box of `44px × 44px`.
- **Card Media Standards**: Food and recipe visual assets follow a strict `4:3` aspect ratio to standardize vertical rhythm and prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).

## Elevation & Depth

### Organic Layering & Tinted Ambient Elevation
This design system rejects generic cool-gray shadows. Elevation is achieved through soft, tinted ambient occlusion derived from `ink-900` (`rgba(20, 32, 26, 0.06)`), creating a natural paper-on-linen separation.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `surface-muted` (`#F7FAF8`) or `surface-sunken` (`#EEF3F0`) for recessed search bars, tab tracks, and quantity steppers.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Stack)**: `surface-default` (`#FFFFFF`) with border `1px solid #E6ECE8` and ambient drop:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(20, 32, 26, 0.04), 0 4px 16px rgba(20, 32, 26, 0.06);`
  Applied to meal cards, nutrition stat widgets, and grocery item modules.
- **Level 2 (Floating Overlays & Drawers)**:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px rgba(20, 32, 26, 0.05), 0 12px 32px rgba(20, 32, 26, 0.12);`
  Applied to mobile bottom sheets, sticky cart action bars, and allergen warning dialogs.
- **Focus Rings**: Pure accessibility focus standard utilizing `ring-2 ring-brand-500` offset by `2px` of pure white.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Roundedness
The system implements a friendly, approachable geometric language defined by soft, organic radii:
- **Base Components (`rounded-md`, 8px)**: Segmented controls, input fields, step counters, and micro action buttons.
- **Structural Modules (`rounded-xl`, 14px)**: Secondary dialogs, flyout popovers, recipe ingredient tables, and sub-panels.
- **Signature Cards & Sheets (`rounded-2xl`, 20px / 1.25rem)**: The brand hallmark applied across primary recipe cards, nutritional macro breakdown dashboards, meal calendar containers, and modal sheets.
- **Pills & Continuous Chips (`rounded-full`)**: Applied strictly to status tags, allergen clearance indicators, nutritional diet filters (e.g., "Keto", "Low FODMAP"), and macro markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Interactive (`brand-600`)**: Solid `#1B8553` fill with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl` (14px), 44px min-height, font `label-lg`. Transitions to `#176A44` on hover/active. Used for "Lên thực đơn" (Plan Meals) and "Xác nhận" (Confirm).
- **Commerce Action (`accent-500`)**: Solid `#F58A2B` fill with white text, transitioning to `#DC6D12` on hover. Used exclusively for grocery delivery actions: "Thêm vào giỏ" (Add to Cart) and "Đặt hàng nguyên liệu" (Order Groceries).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Outline `1px solid #E6ECE8`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `ink-700` (`#34443B`). On hover: background `#F0FBF4`, border `brand-300`.

### Cards (Recipe & Grocery)
- **Recipe Card**: Built with `rounded-2xl` (20px), base `#FFFFFF`, and `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(20,32,26,0.06), 0 4px 16px rgba(20,32,26,0.06)`. Houses a `4:3` aspect ratio cover image with a floating heart icon (`♥`) top right (touch target 44×44px). The lower section features cooking time, calorie tally, and dynamic health status badges.
- **Grocery Cart Item**: Shallow row container with `surface-default`, thumbnail image (1:1 ratio, 56×56px), title, weight specification in `ink-500`, and quantity stepper (`−` / `+`).

### Health Status Tags & Verdict Badges
Health badges require both background-foreground pairing and explicit glyphs:
- **Fit / Compatible (`ok`)**: Background `#E8F7EE`, Foreground `#15703F`, Glyph `✓` ("Phù hợp").
- **Caution / Adjust (`warn`)**: Background `#FFF4D6`, Foreground `#8A5A00`, Glyph `⚠` ("Lưu ý lượng muối").
- **Allergy Conflict / Incompatible (`danger`)**: Background `#FDE8E8`, Foreground `#A11A1A`, Glyph `⛔` ("Chứa đậu phộng").

### Nutrition Progress Rings (`MacroRing`)
- Centered concentric SVG progress rings or segmented donuts displaying total kcal alongside macro splits:
  - Blue (`#3B82F6`) for Protein
  - Amber (`#F59E0B`) for Carbs
  - Rose (`#EC4899`) for Fat
  - Mint (`#10B981`) for Fiber
- Background track set to `surface-sunken` (`#EEF3F0`) with 4px stroke width.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Text Inputs**: `surface-default` with `1px solid #A5B3AA`, `rounded-xl`, 44px height, padding `space-md`. Focus transitions to `ring-2 ring-brand-500` with border `#27A468`.
- **Servings & Quantity Stepper**: Compact horizontal pill capsule using `surface-sunken` (`#EEF3F0`), containing circular flat buttons (`44px` target area) with crisp `+` and `−` symbols and bold tabular numbers in `ink-900`.
